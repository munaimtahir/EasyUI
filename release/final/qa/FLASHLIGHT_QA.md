# Flashlight QA

1. Use a phone with flash hardware.
2. Open the EasyUI home screen.
3. Tap the flashlight tile.
4. Confirm the flashlight turns on or, if unsupported on that device, the app fails gracefully without crashing.
5. Tap again and confirm it turns off or again fails gracefully.

Pass if there is no crash and the tile behaves honestly on the device.
