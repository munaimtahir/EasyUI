# Shot List By Screen

1. `IntroScreen`
   - Capture the title `EasyUI Senior Launcher`
   - Keep `Start Setup` visible

2. `DefaultLauncherGuidanceScreen`
   - Capture `Set EasyUI as Home`
   - Keep `Open Default App Settings` visible if the device is not already using EasyUI

3. `HomeScreen`
   - Capture clock, date, home tiles, and `Caregiver Tools`

4. `AppListScreen`
   - Capture `All Apps`
   - Enter a short search term in `Search apps`

5. `CaregiverToolsScreen`
   - Capture the `Protection` and `Home stability` cards

6. `FavoriteContactsScreen`
   - Capture `Favorite Contacts`
   - Show one realistic sample contact with photo or placeholder

7. `HomeDisplayScreen`
   - Capture `Home Readability`
   - Keep one preset selection button visible

8. `HiddenAppsScreen`
   - Capture `Show or Hide Apps`
   - Keep quick presets visible
