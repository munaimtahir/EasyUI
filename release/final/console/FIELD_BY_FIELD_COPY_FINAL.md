# Field By Field Copy

## Main listing

- App name:
  - `EasyUI Senior Launcher`
- Short description:
  - `Simple senior-friendly launcher with large buttons and caregiver setup tools.`
- Full description:
  - `EasyUI Senior Launcher is a simpler Android home screen for seniors and the people who help set up their phones.`
  - `It replaces a cluttered launcher with large buttons, clearer labels, and a steadier daily layout. The goal is simple: make the phone easier to read, easier to tap, easier to navigate, and harder to accidentally mess up.`
  - `EasyUI is built for practical everyday use:`
  - `- large home tiles for the apps and actions that matter most`
  - `- a clear All Apps list with search`
  - `- caregiver setup tools for a calmer home screen`
  - `- favorite contact tiles that open the phone dialer`
  - `- optional flashlight tile on supported devices`
  - `- hidden-app filtering inside EasyUI`
  - `- local-first setup with no account required`
  - `EasyUI focuses on clarity and stability, not Android lockdown. It does not claim enterprise device control, remote monitoring, or full system restrictions.`
  - `Why families choose EasyUI:`
  - `- seniors get a cleaner, easier home screen`
  - `- caregivers get a simpler setup path`
  - `- the layout stays more stable for daily use`
  - `- the app works offline and keeps its setup data on the device`
  - `EasyUI Senior Launcher is a consumer launcher designed to reduce confusion, reduce accidental changes, and make phones easier to hand over with confidence.`
- Category:
  - `Personalization`
- Tags:
  - `launcher`, `seniors`, `accessibility`, `simple phone`, `caregiver`

## Reviewer communication

- Reviewer notes:
  - `No login or account is required.`
  - `Reviewer path:`
  - `1. Launch the app.`
  - `2. Tap Start Setup.`
  - `3. Review Set EasyUI as Home.`
  - `4. Continue into Caregiver Help.`
  - `5. Finish setup and open the Home screen.`
  - `6. Open Caregiver Tools from the bottom button on the home screen.`
  - `Important behavior notes:`
  - `- This is a consumer launcher, not kiosk or device-owner software.`
  - `- Favorite contact tiles open the system dialer with ACTION_DIAL; they do not place calls automatically.`
  - `- Flashlight support depends on device flash hardware.`
  - `- Hidden apps affect visibility inside EasyUI only. They do not block the apps elsewhere on the phone.`
