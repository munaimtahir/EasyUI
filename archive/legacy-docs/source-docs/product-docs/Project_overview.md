# EasyUI Senior Launcher

EasyUI Senior Launcher is an Android launcher designed to help seniors use smartphones more comfortably and with fewer mistakes.

The product is built around a simple principle:
make the phone easier to see, easier to understand, and harder to accidentally mess up.

This app is intended primarily for caregivers and family members who repeatedly help older relatives fix common phone problems such as:
- moved or deleted icons
- confusing app clutter
- difficulty finding key apps
- accidental opening of settings
- poor readability due to small text or crowded layouts
- repeated calls for tech support

The launcher will provide a simple home screen, large visual targets, clear labels, photo-based contacts, and caregiver protection features such as PIN-protected editing, app hiding, and layout locking.

The product is offline-first and should avoid unnecessary backend complexity.

## Product philosophy
- simple before powerful
- stable before customizable
- trust before growth hacks
- caregiver-first setup
- low-maintenance architecture
- honest technical boundaries

## Non-goals
This app will not promise full device lockdown on normal consumer phones.
It will not market itself as a kiosk shell or enterprise device-control tool.
It will not require a cloud account to function.
