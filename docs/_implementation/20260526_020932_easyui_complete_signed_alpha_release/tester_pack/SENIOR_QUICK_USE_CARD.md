# EasyUI Senior Quick Use Card

Welcome to your new phone screen! We've made it simpler so you can easily reach what matters most.

*   **To make a call:** Tap the big "Phone" button.
*   **To see your photos:** Tap "Photos".
*   **To see all your other apps:** Tap "More Apps".
*   **If you get lost:** Just press the **Home** button at the bottom of your phone. It will always bring you back to this simple screen.
*   **If you need help:** Tap the "Emergency" button or ask your caregiver to check your phone.
