# EasyUI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 26, 2026

EasyUI Senior Launcher is built with a "Privacy by Design" and "Offline-First" philosophy. We believe your phone usage is your business.

## 1. Data Collection
EasyUI **does not collect** any personal information, location data, app usage statistics, or telemetry. No data is transmitted to our servers because we do not have any servers.

## 2. Data Storage
All configuration data (PINs, hidden apps, layout settings) is stored **locally on your device** using Android's internal database (Room) and DataStore. This data is never synced to the cloud.

## 3. Permissions
EasyUI requests standard Android permissions to function as a launcher and safety tool:
- **Phone/Calling:** To allow seniors to call emergency contacts in one tap.
- **App Management:** To display and launch your installed apps.
- **Battery/Network:** To monitor "Phone Health" and alert the senior if the battery is low or internet is disconnected.

## 4. Third-Party Services
EasyUI does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics (like Google Analytics), or crash reporting (like Firebase) that would transmit data off the device.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
Any changes to this policy will be communicated through app updates. Since we do not collect contact info, we cannot notify you directly.
