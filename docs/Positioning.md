# Positioning

## Primary positioning statement
A simple Android launcher for seniors, designed to reduce mistakes and make phones easier to use.

## Stronger buyer-facing positioning
Stop accidental taps, moved icons, and confusing screens for your parents.

## Audience
Primary:
- adult children helping parents
- caregivers setting up phones for seniors

Secondary:
- seniors who want bigger and simpler controls
- digital minimalists who like high-clarity interfaces

## Key messaging pillars
- easier to read
- easier to tap
- easier to keep stable
- easier for family to maintain
- private and offline-first

## App store naming direction
EasyUI Senior Launcher
or
EasyUI: Senior Phone Launcher

## Screenshot messaging ideas
1. Big buttons. Clear text.
2. Simpler screen for seniors.
3. Lock the layout from accidental changes.
4. Hide confusing apps.
5. Call family with one tap.
