# Risks and Assumptions

## Key assumptions

- caregivers are the main decision-makers
- users value simplicity over flexibility
- a one-time payment fits the buyer better than a subscription
- offline-first architecture improves trust and lowers maintenance

## Main risks

### Wrong positioning

If the app is marketed as full lockdown, expectations will become unrealistic.

### Weak differentiation

Large icons alone are not enough. The product must win on caregiver stability features.

### Over-expansion

Adding too many settings will make the launcher less senior-friendly.

### OEM inconsistency

Launcher behavior and default-app flows vary across Android devices.

## Mitigation

- honest promise
- strong setup guidance
- clear premium value
- narrow, stable scope
