# Product Guardrails

## Features rejected for MVP and v1

- system-level notification shade disable
- guaranteed settings restriction at OS level
- enterprise kiosk promises
- device policy enforcement on consumer devices
- cloud account dependency
- remote web dashboard
- cross-device sync
- AI assistant inside the launcher
- complex gesture systems
- ad-supported clutter

## Guardrails

- Do not market the app as full lockdown.
- Do not imply enterprise device management.
- Do not depend on manufacturer-specific hacks as core value.
- Do not add features that make caregiver setup harder.
- Do not prioritize customization over stability.

## Product truth

This app simplifies the home experience and reduces common accidental changes. It does not fully control Android system behavior across all consumer devices.
